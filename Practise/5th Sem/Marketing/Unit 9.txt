                                                                      UNIT 9 - Promotion Decisions
                   
# Concept of Promotion:

    => All the activities and tools that help companies communicate with the customers and inform them about their products.
    => Influence the sales and distribution of the company's product by convincing the customer to use their product.

# Objectives of Promotion: (IPRER)

    1. Informing - provide information about the use, benefits and features of the product
    2. Persuading - only informing is not enough, they also need to kindly persuade and appeal them to use their products
    3. Reminding - reminding consumers about the presence of the product in the market since similar products are available in the market
    4. Entertaining - customer aren't interested in plain advertising and promotion, they seek entertainment. So use music, arts, song, dance, 
                      attractive figures to entertain and attract them
    5. re-enforcing - if above mentioned objectives are ineffective, use psychological pressure to compel them in purchasing or re-purchasing their product

# Concept of Promotion Mix Components (Tools):

    => The right combination of promotion tools used by a company to communicate with their audience about the products.

# Some of the Promotion Mix Tools: (APSPD)

    1. Advertising - promotional activity using variety of media such as radio, television, magazines, newspapers, leaflets, brochures
                     posters, etc to sell the products to target audience
    2. Personal Selling - face-to-face communication with the customer using the sales person's interpersonal skill to persuade them in buying
                          their product. Individual attention to each consumer
    3. Sales Promotion - providing incentives such as discount, vouchers, coupons, contests, samples, etc to encourage customers in buying 
                         their products. Stimulate consumer demand for the product and services
    4. Public Relation - strategic communication process that aims to build mutually beneficial relationships between the company and the public.
                         By maintaining good image in the society and seek acceptance from the public.
                   '
    5. Direct Marketing - Communicating directly with the customers and potential customers to promote their products and services.
                          Using various means such as email, text messages, mail, phone call, SMS, etc
                        
# Factors affecting determination of Promotion Mix (Tools): (NPSFP)

    1. Nature of Market:

        a) Geographic Coverage:
            
            -> Small Market - personal selling
            -> Mass Marketing - advertising

        b) Types of customer: 

            -> Middlemen - personal selling
            -> Consumer - advertising

        c) Market Concentration:

            => The total number of prospective buyers in the particular area.
            -> High - advertising and public relation
            -> Low - personal selling
    
    2. Product Factors:

        a) Unit value:

            -> Expensive item (jewelry) - personal selling
            -> Less expensive items - advertising

        b) Nature of product:

            -> Perishable and technical products (milk, vegetables, meat) - personal selling
            -> Non-perishable and general products (toothpaste, batteries, rice) - advertising
            -> Pre-sale and post-sale service - personal selling

    3. Stage of the product life cycle

        a) Introduction stage:

            -> Potential customer - advertising, sales promotion
            -> Middlemen - personal selling and sales promotion
        
        b) Growth and maturity stage:

            -> Sales promotion heavy focus. Advertising to support sales promotion.

        c) Decline stage:

            -> No need of any promotion.

    4. Availability of fund:

        => Advertising requires heavy funding and resources. Effectiveness depend on how much financial resource is invested.

    5. Promotional Strategy:

        a) Push strategy:

            => Directed at middlemen
            => Manufacturer (persuades) -> wholesaler (to carry product. Then convince) -> Retailer (to sell. Again, pushes) -> Customer (to buy product)
            => Personal selling used.

        b) Pull strategy:

            => Directed at customer
            => Customer demand product -> retailers plan the order to wholesalers -> wholesalers plan the order to manufacturers

# Concept of Advertising:

    => Promotional activity to sell a product or service to a target audience. 
    => Use variety of media such as radio, television, newspapers, brochures, leaflets, posters, symbols, etc.
    => Target large numbers of potential customers.
    => Paid communication.

# Features/Nature of Advertising: (MIPCPIN)

    1. Mass communication - target large audience
    2. Informative - disseminate company and product information
    3. Persuasive - AIDA (Attention, Interest, Desire, Desire to action
    4. Competitive - used as competitive weapon
    5. Paid Activity - requires financial investment
    6. Identified sponsor - either the producer or the dealers on behalf of the producers. They are known through their name, logo and brand.
    7. Non-personal communication - no face-to-face contact with the customer. Use appropriate media.

# Objectives of Advertising: (ISMAV)

    1. Introduce a product
    2. Spread awareness
    3. Maintain existing market position
    4. Acquiring customers
    5. Value creation

# Media of Advertising: (BPOOSM)

    1. Broadcast media - TV, radio
    2. Printed media - newspapers, magazines, posters
    3. Outdoor media - hoardings and bill boards
    4. Online media - social media, website
    5. Specialty media - items given to people that contains name and brand of the company such as 
                         membership cards, cups, carry bags, key rings, etc
    6. Mobile - SMS, social media

# Concept of Personal Selling:

    => Face-to-face communication. 
    => Use salesperson interpersonal skills
    => Individual attention for each consumer

# Features/Nature of Personal Selling: (PDFOR)

    1. Personal form - face-to-face or talk through phone or internet
    2. Development of relationship - two way communication
    3. Flexible - tailor marketing message based on the user need and behavior
    4. Oral communication - oral means
    5. Real sale - not only inform but also sell the product in most cases

# Process of Personal Selling: 

    A) Indoor sales process - inside the store (ARIDSOC)

        1. Drawing Attention - decorations, sign boards, proper display of goods
        2. Reception - kind and welcoming reception of the customers
        3. Inquiry - ask them about their needs and desires
        4. Demonstration of goods - introduce all brand and quality. comparative merits and demerits of the alternative products
        5. Handling objections - give tactful answers to customer questions and objections
        6. Selection of goods - cooperative and make them feel their selection is good
        7. Closing the sales - inform them about the future products before they leave

    B) Outdoor sales process - Find the potential customers themselves (PPADOCF)

        1. Prospecting - make a list of qualified customers (those who have need, can buy it and have the authority)
                       - through newspapers, personal observation, trade publications, family and friends

        2. Pre-approach - gain additional information such as hobby, lifestyle, consuming pattern, income, education

        3. Approach - approach and inform about company, self and product

        4. Product demonstration 

        5. Handling objections

        6. Closing the sales - gain knowledge about their special preferences for future reference

        7. Follow up - make sure all the questions and problems faced by the customers are resolved

# Concept of Sales Promotions:

    => Stimulate demand for product using incentives such as discount, coupons, vouchers, prizes, contests, samples, etc.

# Features/Natures of Sales Promotion: (SIQT)

    1. Short term
    2. Provide incentives
    3. Aim for quick response
    4. Primarily directed at target audience (who buy the goods, product resellers, sales forces)

# Objectives Fo Sales Promotion: (ASDREO)

    1. Attract new customers
    2. Stimulate demand
    3. Brand differentiation
    4. Reward loyal customers
    5. Create excitement towards the product
    6. Promote off-season products
